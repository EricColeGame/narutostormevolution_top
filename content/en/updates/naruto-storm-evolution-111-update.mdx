## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the Naruto Storm Evolution 1.11 update mandatory?

If you play online, yes — mismatched versions can prevent you from connecting to other players. If you only play offline, you can delay, but you will eventually need it to stay compatible with saved setups and community content built on the newer version.

### Will the Naruto Storm Evolution 1.11 update erase my progress?

Community reports do not indicate save wipes, but backing up your save folder before patching takes under a minute and removes all risk. Do it every time, not just for major versions.

### Do I need to reinstall my mods after updating?

Usually yes. Many mods are built against a specific version, and files can conflict after a patch. Remove them first, confirm the game runs cleanly, then reintroduce them one at a time.

### Why does the game crash right after I patch?

The most common causes are an interrupted download, a leftover mod file, or corrupted local data. Verify your game files through your launcher, clear your mod folder, and launch offline once before going online.
