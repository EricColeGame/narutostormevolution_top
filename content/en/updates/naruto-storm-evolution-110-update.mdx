## Troubleshooting Common Update Problems

Not every install goes smoothly. Most issues fall into a handful of predictable categories, and most have simple fixes.

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Download stalls at a fixed percentage | Store or launcher cache issue | Pause, restart the client, resume |
| Version mismatch in online lobbies | Update not fully applied | Re-verify files and relaunch |
| Crash on startup | Old mod files or shader cache | Disable mods, clear cache, retry |
| Missing characters or costumes | Partial asset download | Re-download the content pack |
| Stutter or frame drops after patching | Rebuilding shaders on first run | Play a few matches, then re-test |
| Save data appears reset | Profile sync conflict | Restore from your backup, then re-sync |

If a problem persists after two clean attempts, report it with your platform, version number, and a short clip. Bug reports with video get triaged far faster than text-only complaints.
