## Who Is Naruto Storm Evolution Konan?

Konan is the Akatsuki's only female member and the partner of Pain, also known as Nagato, in the Naruto story. In arena fighters built on the Storm formula, that background translates into a very specific gameplay identity: she is a technical zoner with unusual aerial freedom, not a brawler who wins by trading hits at close range.

Most players who try her for the first time make the same mistake — they rush in and try to play her like a close-combat combo character. That is not where her strengths live. Her value comes from controlling space, forcing opponents to approach on her terms, and then punishing that approach.

The table below summarizes the tendencies that player experience reports most consistently across community builds.

| Trait | Typical Konan Behavior | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Effective range | Mid to long | Keeps her outside brawler range |
| Mobility | Strong aerial drift and glide | Escapes ground pressure and resets neutral |
| Damage per hit | Moderate, spikes in full combos | Rewards clean confirms over button mashing |
| Durability | Low raw health, high evasion | Punishes sloppy approaches hard |
| Execution difficulty | Moderate to high | Requires spacing discipline |
| Ideal role | Zoner and support-swap specialist | Controls the pace of the match |

### A Quick Lore Refresher That Explains Her Kit

Konan grew up in Amegakure, the Village Hidden in the Rain, orphaned by war and trained alongside Nagato and Yahiko under Jiraiya. Her signature ability is Shikigami no Mai, the Dance of the Shikigami, which lets her dissolve her body into sheets of paper and reshape it into wings, spears, clones, and shuriken. If you want the full canonical breakdown of her techniques and history, the [Naruto wiki page on Konan](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Konan) is a solid reference.

That lore matters for gameplay. Jiraiya famously countered her paper with oil-based attacks, and her paper constructs are conceptually vulnerable to anything that saturates or burns them. Many arena fighters echo that idea through fire, water, and oil-flavored techniques, so expect those matchups to feel harder than the raw numbers suggest. For broader context on the Storm-style fighting games she is modeled after, Bandai Namco's [official Naruto game hub](https://www.bandainamcoent.com/) is the place to start.

## Konan's Toolkit: Jutsu, Awakening, and Paper Ninjutsu

Her move set is built around three ideas: long-range harassment, aerial repositioning, and a finishing sequence that converts a clean hit into a round. Community reports describe her as a character who wins the neutral game first and the damage race second.

| Tool | What It Does | Best Use |
|---|---|---|
| Paper projectile volley | Fast, low-commitment ranged poke | Chip damage, whiff punishment, testing approach habits |
| Piercing paper thrust | Longer reach than most normals | Mid-range punish after a baited dash |
| Paper clone escape | Trades position for safety | Escaping corner pressure without burning a substitution |
| Paper wings / glide | Alters air angle and hang time | Baiting anti-airs, crossing up landings |
| Awakening form | Denser projectiles, faster movement | Closing out a round when you already have momentum |
| Ultimate technique | Screen-filling explosive tag sequence | Combo ender after a juggle or a hard read |

Two details separate average Konan players from dangerous ones. First, her projectiles are a tool for creating reactions, not for dealing damage — you throw them to make the opponent move, then punish the movement. Second, her aerial mobility is a defensive resource. Floating above the ground forces grounded characters to guess where you will land, and every wrong guess is a free punish.

In the source material, her most famous technique turns the battlefield into an ocean of explosive tags. That spectacle usually carries over into her ultimate animation, which is why players often save meter for a guaranteed kill rather than spending it on chip damage.

## How to Play Naruto Storm Evolution Konan: Step-by-Step Combo Plan

The following sequence is a general framework that works across most builds. Adjust the exact inputs to your control scheme, but keep the order of operations intact.

| Step | Action | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Backdash and throw paper projectiles | Establish distance and read the opponent's approach |
| 2 | Sidestep or glide after the second projectile | Bait a dash or a whiffed rush |
| 3 | Punish with the piercing paper thrust | Start your confirm from maximum safety |
| 4 | Air juggle with light, fast hits | Build meter without overextending |
| 5 | Call a support assist to extend | Keep the combo alive while saving your own resources |
| 6 | Finish with the ultimate | Convert a single opening into a round |

A few supporting habits make this plan far more reliable:

- **Budget your meter.** Hold enough for one ultimate plus one defensive escape. Spending everything on chip damage leaves you with no way out of pressure.
- **Save your substitution.** Use the paper clone escape when you can, and keep the standard substitution for situations where the clone would not create enough space.
- **Delay the awakening.** Popping it early feels powerful but wastes its enhanced movement. Activate it when the opponent is already low and forced to approach.
- **Watch the clock.** Konan wins long rounds. If you are ahead on health, slowing the pace down is a legitimate strategy, not a cowardly one.

## Best Support Picks and Team Synergy for Konan

Because Konan's damage comes from clean confirms rather than raw pressure, the best supports for her are the ones that either hold an opponent still or cover her recovery. Support naming varies between community builds, so the table below focuses on archetypes and the kind of fighter that usually fills them.

| Support Archetype | Example Character Type | What It Adds | When to Call |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lockdown assist | Large-area stagger fighter | Holds the opponent for your ultimate | Right after a successful air juggle |
| Rushdown assist | Fast close-range attacker | Covers the gap when you must approach | When the opponent refuses to leave the corner |
| Defensive counter | Counter or parry specialist | Interrupts aggressive rushdown | Mid-blockstring, before a knockdown |
| Ranged pressure | Projectile-heavy fighter | Doubles your zoning threat | Neutral game, to force a bad approach |
| Utility or heal | Support-focused fighter | Extends your survivability | Between rounds of pressure |

Player experience suggests that lockdown plus rushdown is the most forgiving pairing for newer players. It gives you an answer both when the opponent runs away and when they refuse to stop attacking. More advanced players often prefer double ranged pressure, which turns neutral into a war of attrition Konan is favored to win.

## Matchup Cheat Sheet and Practice Drills

Knowing which fights to slow down and which to speed up is half the battle. This table reflects community-reported tendencies rather than guaranteed results.

| Opponent Type | Difficulty | Game Plan | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rushdown brawlers | Hard | Stay airborne, reset with projectiles | Standing your ground in the corner |
| Zoners | Even | Win the projectile war, then change air angles | Trading shots from a fixed position |
| Grapplers | Favorable | Zone them out, never land beside them | Ending combos in grab range |
| Balanced all-rounders | Even | Out-patience them, win the meter race | Forcing early aggression |
| Counter specialists | Hard | Feint and bait, then punish the counter | Predictable projectile timing |

The most common mistakes Konan players make are easy to fix once you can name them.

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Playing her like a rushdown character | She loses every trade at close range | Start every exchange from mid-range |
| Throwing projectiles on autopilot | Opponents walk in behind predictable shots | Throw with a purpose, then reposition |
| Burning the ultimate early | You lose your best round-ending tool | Save it for a confirmed combo |
| Ignoring air options | You give up her biggest advantage | Practice glide movement in training mode |

A short daily routine goes a long way. Spend ten minutes on spacing drills, keeping your opponent at the exact distance where your paper thrust connects and their normals do not. Spend five minutes on substitution reactions, letting a training dummy attack while you practice escaping. Then review one replay and count how many times you threw a projectile without a follow-up plan — that single habit is usually worth more than any combo upgrade.

## FAQ

**Is Naruto Storm Evolution Konan good for beginners?**
She is playable but not the easiest starting pick. Her strengths are spacing and patience, which take time to learn. If you already understand neutral game fundamentals from other Storm-style fighters, she is a great choice; if you are brand new, consider a straightforward brawler first.

**What are Konan's biggest weaknesses in Naruto Storm Evolution?**
Low durability and weak close-range trades. Anything that closes distance quickly and stays there is a problem, and techniques with fire, oil, or water themes tend to feel stronger against her paper constructs. Keep your distance and use the air to reset.

**How do I unlock Konan?**
Availability depends on the build you are running. In most community versions she is part of the standard roster, but roster changes happen between updates, so check the current patch notes or the community channel that distributes your build.

**What is the best support for Konan?**
A lockdown assist that holds the opponent in place for your ultimate is the most reliable choice. Pair it with a rushdown assist so you still have an answer when the opponent tries to run away and stall the clock.

Get comfortable with her spacing first, and the damage will follow. Naruto Storm Evolution Konan rewards the kind of player who would rather win a war of patience than a race of reflexes — and once that clicks, she becomes one of the most satisfying characters on the roster.
