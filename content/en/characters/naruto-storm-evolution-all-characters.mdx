| Archetype | Signature Traits | Strengths | Watch Out For |

### Stats Are a Starting Point, Not a Verdict

Attack and defense numbers tell you what a character does on paper. They do not tell you how the fighter feels in your hands. A slightly lower-damage character with a fast, reliable combo route will often outperform a high-damage fighter whose timing you cannot hit consistently. Test in training, then trust your results.

## Team Building: Making Characters Work Together

The lead fighter gets the highlight reel, but the support slot decides most close matches. When you look at Naruto Storm Evolution all characters, think in pairs rather than individuals.

| Team Archetype | Lead Character Type | Recommended Support Type | Why It Works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Balanced control | All-rounder | Defensive or counter assist | Covers mistakes, keeps neutral stable |
| Rush pressure | Fast brawler | Ranged or trapping assist | Forces opponents into your short range |
| Zone lockdown | Ranged zoner | Disruption assist | Punishes opponents who finally close distance |
| Counter and punish | Bruiser | Fast interrupt assist | Turns blocked pressure into damage |
| Awakening burst | High-damage form | Combo-extending assist | Maximizes your one big window |

The most common mistake is stacking two characters who want the same distance. If both of your fighters only function up close, a single well-played zoner can shut down your entire team.

### Assist Timing Tips That Pay Off Immediately

- Call your assist **after** you confirm a hit, not before — whiffed assists are free punish opportunities.
- Use defensive assists when you are being pressured, not when you are already in a combo.
- Save your strongest support for the final round if the matchup favors you.
- Practice the same assist call in training until the timing is muscle memory.

## FAQ

**Is Naruto Storm Evolution all characters available from the start?**
No. A portion of the roster is available immediately, while many fighters are tied to story progress, mission objectives, events, or in-game currency. Expect to unlock gradually rather than in one sitting.

**Do support characters matter as much as the lead?**

**How often does the roster change?**
